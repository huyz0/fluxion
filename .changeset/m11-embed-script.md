`@fluxion/player-inline` also builds `dist/fluxion-player.js`: a classic script that defines `<fluxion-player>` over the same opening as the one-file player (lean reader, bundled shapes, the file's images and fonts). `examples/embed/index.html` shows a page that uses it. The one-file player and the element share one `openFlux`.
