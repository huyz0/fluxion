`O` opens an overview grid over the deck (FR-PRS-002): every visible screen as a small drawing of itself in presentation order, the current one marked; a click on one goes to it, Escape or `O` closes it on the screen it was opened on, the arrow keys, Home and End move focus among the thumbnails, and focus returns to where it was. `DeckAction` gains the `overview` kind.
