Used-glyph font subsetting: the glyph inventory of a document, pinned HarfBuzz and WOFF2 engines, and a cached plan that turns each embedded font into a validated WOFF2 subset or keeps it whole with a reason (Reserved Font Name, variable font, collection, malformed, too large, not smaller, unbounded text). Saving with it comes in a later release.
