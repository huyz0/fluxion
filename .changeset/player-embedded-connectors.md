The one-file player and the player element register the pack connector types a `.flux` or `.flux.html` embeds (`connectors/<pack>.json`), so their connectors draw the same decoration as in the studio and the routing worker routes the orthogonal-based ones; a refused entry draws its connectors as an unknown type and never fails the open. `@fluxion/format/player` exports `embeddedConnectors` and `EmbeddedConnectors`.
